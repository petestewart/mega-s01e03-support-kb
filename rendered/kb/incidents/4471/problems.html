The problems

Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first gets day and month swapped, through any channel. Every tenant on file writes the day first.

Hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. This happens only on the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. Their app and email reports are parser reports.

One-day moves. These reports show a date one day off, or a different day for two people looking at the same meeting. A swap of day and month cannot do that. The cause is not proven: time zone conversion is the first thing to check, and the export holds no data that confirms it.

How to place a report

Go by the tenant and the channel, as causes.ts does. Do not use the intake tag: it follows the reporter’s browser, so it marks day-first tenants on en-US browsers as hint. Do not use the subject line either, because the same subject appears on reports with different causes (see triage).
